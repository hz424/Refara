# Evidence timing

The analyses were specified at different stages:

- The study team fixed the held-out GSE162632 roots, tasks, configurations,
  reference constructions, utility and complete 84-member family before first
  inspecting that family; it was not preregistered or fixed by an external
  protocol.
- The focal contrast was selected after the complete 84-member family had been
  inspected.
- The separate 56-member family of paired shared-to-split and
  shared-to-rotation contrast changes was specified after the results were
  inspected. Its diagnostic bounds and targeted calibration were reported separately
  from the original 84-member family. The calibration failed its all-scenario
  criterion; these are not calibrated confidence intervals, and no joint
  140-member error-control claim is made.
- The broader PBMC allocation and alternative-metric analyses are post hoc
  complete-family sensitivity analyses.
- The program-level consequence analysis was specified after the ranking
  results were known but before its program readouts were calculated. It is an
  exploratory extension rather than a preregistered analysis.
- The observed IFN-gamma cell-type contrast was fixed before expression from
  the 39 unused donors was extracted. Those donors were absent from model
  fitting and candidate screening, but share the study and experimental
  batches with the discovery data.
- The Sound Life analysis is post hoc and graph-conditional.
- Metadata or protocol cases that did not meet the stated requirements were
  not analysed directionally.

The unused-donor analysis tests a fixed observed-response contrast within the
same study; it is not an external replication of the full model-selection chain.
