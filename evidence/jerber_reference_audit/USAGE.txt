Run from any directory:
  python /path/to/evidence/jerber_reference_audit/replay.py --output /new/output/directory

Requires Python and NumPy. No network, weights or original filesystem is needed.
The replay recomputes means, margins, directions, ties and allocation Monte
Carlo errors from frozen losses. It checks all 7,680 root-task-pair-allocation
rows, 384 task-pair-allocation rows and 24 integration summaries against the
bundled tables. It does not rerun model inference or expression preprocessing.

The diagnostic covers 20 previously analysed roots, 8 tasks and 16 prespecified
allocations. Root indices preserve the original panel; shared pool and culture
dependence remains. Results describe this fixed panel, with no independent
experimental validation, population confidence interval or reporting-rule
advantage claimed.

Average the two complementary orientations before computing Monte Carlo errors.
The mean O score and the mean two-fold crossfit score are equal by construction.

The S24 versus O12/12 diagnostic uses corrected scGen context dispatch. The
historical S24, D8/8/8 and three-rotation D correction is separate. The manifest
records source hashes, attribution and expected counts. Source-data terms remain
in force; see the repository LICENSE_SCOPE.md.
